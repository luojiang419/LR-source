# SHIYING 品牌标识

桌面名称：**SHIYING 数字资产**；安卓名称：**SHIYING 库存管理**。

标识以连续 S 形带连接叠放的图片资产卡，表达资产采集、归档与流转。主色为鼠尾草绿 `#c6dda5`，辅助色为暖金 `#d8ab78`，背景透明。

使用内置 image_gen 生成原始设计稿，并经过一次去除光晕的修订；原稿保存在 `SHIYING-logo原始设计.png`。正式程序采用人工整理的平面 SVG `../演示方案/brand.svg`，保证小尺寸清晰、可重复构建，避免原始位图的光照与边缘噪点进入程序图标。

运行 `python -X utf8 tools/build_brand_assets.py` 从同一 SVG 生成 Windows 多尺寸 ICO / PNG、Android launcher PNG，以及桌面和安卓页面使用的 SVG。托盘和安装器共用 Windows 图标。修改 Logo 后先执行此脚本，再构建桌面、安装工具和 APK。

## 原始生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production application icon master for SHIYING digital asset management and inventory management software. Create ONE highly distinctive premium minimalist geometric symbol, centered on a perfectly square canvas, transparent background. The symbol should synthesize a continuous angular S ribbon with two offset archival image tiles / stacked asset cards, suggesting orderly storage, image curation and flow. Precisely constructed, clean vector-like silhouette with generous negative space, broad crisp strokes, subtly chamfered corners. Quiet confident enterprise creative tool, timeless Swiss graphic design. Two flat colors only: pale sage #c6dda5 for the main ribbon and muted warm brass #d8ab78 for one small structural accent. Shape fills about 76 percent of square, with 12 percent safe margin on all sides. Clearly readable at 16px, balanced optical weight. No letters or text, no wordmark, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no photo, no texture, no border, no background panel, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no multiple alternatives. Actual transparent alpha background. Design a finished icon that can be placed on charcoal #202723 or warm ivory app surfaces.

## 修订要求

保留 S 形和叠放图片卡结构，清除光晕、阴影和纹理；使用透明背景、清晰边缘、鼠尾草绿与暖金，保留安全边距，不加文字。
